## Taxonomic sources

- Kästle & Regalado Fernández (2025), *Facing the taxonomic impediment — a reassessment of Merulanella*: https://doi.org/10.3897/nhcm.2.144386 . The revision restricts Merulanella and erects new genera using named historical specimens; it does not license an automatic species identification of Ember Bee / Red Diablo trade lines.
- ITIS, *Armadillidium frontetriangulum*: https://www.itis.gov/servlet/SingleRpt/SingleRpt?search_topic=TSN&search_value=594331
- British Myriapod and Isopod Group, *Porcellio laevis*: https://bmig.org.uk/species/porcellio-laevis
- Andalusian terrestrial isopod species treatment, *Porcellio bolivari* and *P. echinatus*: https://www.porcellio.nl/_downloads/aa15b4da958433aa632ac31f65e60872
