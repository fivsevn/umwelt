# 东东的手帐：植物与器物资料

Reviewed 2026-09-30. The garage owns the concise player-facing notebook. `rooftop/botany.mjs` keeps stable botanical identities, aliases, vessel eligibility and source links; `plant-art.mjs` rasterizes characteristic leaves, stems, flowers and fruit. No external photos are embedded or traced.

## Research scope

- Botanical morphology and care: NC State Extension, RHS and NParks species profiles. Genus-wide and container-wide sources are explicitly labeled as shared references in the UI.
- Japanese cultivation: firsthand plant records in 新・花と緑の詳しい図鑑, JA 全農 kitchen-herb guidance, the Japan Gardening Association and Kumamoto University.
- Taiwan: Ministry of Agriculture rooftop vegetable guidance, box cultivation, strawberry crop information, blueberry trials, rosemary cultivation and Taipei mint information. These inform local aliases and climate notes.
- Mainland: iPlant / 中国科学院植物研究所 Flora genus entries provide supplemental Chinese naming and localization. They do not replace cultivation guidance.
- Flowering/fruiting sprites are characteristic illustrative states, not a claim that all plants bloom or fruit in the same season. Outdoor suitability is conditional: frost-tender species need seasonal shelter; cool-season crops and local cultivar choices are noted.

## Vessels and compatibility

Twenty Japanese pottery-inspired designs are original horticultural vessels, not replicas or manufacturer products. Colour, surface and motif references come from 伝統工芸青山スクエア; each design links its corresponding craft page in the garage. The same Arita/Imari tradition has two different designs. Nine everyday choices include red clay, nursery plastic, white plastic, a shallow dish, a foam box, a trough, a grow bag, a deep clay pot and a hanging pot. All planting vessels have drainage.

| Family | Eligibility |
|---|---|
| Cacti / small succulents | Medium drainage pots and shallow dishes; no food boxes |
| Kitchen herbs, onion, strawberry, leaf crops | Ordinary pots, drained foam boxes and troughs |
| Woody plants, large foliage, tomato / chilli, blueberry | Deep pot or grow bag; blueberry uses acidic medium |
| Trailing plants | Hanging pot or shelf-side ordinary pot |
| Medium herbs / flowers / shade foliage | Ordinary drainage pots and pottery-inspired vessels; some flowers also support troughs |

Restrictions are intentionally conservative for the depicted specimen size. They are garden-design choices informed by root habit, not claims that a ceramic tradition is botanically unsuitable. Plant identities remain independent of vessels; choosing a vessel is saved on `object.pot`.

## Rendering and persistence

- All objects are drawn into transparent pixel sprites. A short translucent contact shadow is positioned using the actual raster bounds and the screen orientation. Rotating an object never moves its contact shadow above its artwork. Both maps use native upright coordinates.
- Static sprites and plant/vessel variants use bounded caches. Fish and water animations are refreshed.
- Version 1 layouts remain readable. Valid legacy edge placements with no explicit vessel move minimally inward when their refined footprint needs more room. Truly invalid coordinates and incompatible vessel IDs are rejected.
- Undo/redo, duplication, local persistence, import/export and the upstairs local-layout preview retain vessels. A first vessel change can be undone back to the original default.
