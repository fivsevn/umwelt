**Vernacular names and localization**

- English and Japanese vernacular names are not generated by translating the Chinese display name. Target-language taxonomic databases, research institutions, specialist natural-history references and established trade names are preferred.
- For Porcellio spinicornis, the British Myriapod and Isopod Group explicitly records the common name [Painted Woodlouse](https://bmig.org.uk/species/Porcellio-spinicornis); the English interface uses that name.
- When a stable Japanese common name cannot be verified, the game does not invent one. A Japanese Society of Soil Zoology review supplies Japanese names for several Porcellio species but lists [Porcellio spinicornis](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/edaphologia/95/0/95_KJ00009596263/_pdf/-char/ja) without an attached Japanese vernacular name. The current Japanese specimen page therefore retains the scientific name and may show the verified English common name separately.

**Reference notes**

- Formal taxonomy, distribution and morphology prioritize taxonomic databases, original descriptions, museum or research-institution material, and peer-reviewed literature.
- Undescribed taxa, trade names, locality lines and captive lineages retain their uncertainty; trade designations are not promoted to formal species names.
- Humidity, activity tendencies and compatibility parameters in the game belong to the simulation layer. They are not husbandry advice or biological measurements.
