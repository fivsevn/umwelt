# UMWELT

[中文](README.md) | **English** | [日本語](README_JA.md)

**UMWELT** is a personal experimental project initiated and maintained by **fivsevn**.

The project takes its name from the concept of **Umwelt**, developed by German biologist **Jakob von Uexküll** in his studies of animal perception and behavior. Different organisms do not merely occupy the same abstract “environment”; through their own modes of perception and action, they inhabit meaningful surrounding worlds of their own. This project adopts the concept as a name and intellectual point of departure, while its specific content, visual design, interactive forms, and implementation remain original to the project.

## Isopoda Fugue

**Isopoda Fugue** is a browser-based interactive work centered on isopods. The project draws on materials from biology, natural history, and captive husbandry, and translates selected information into visual and interactive forms.

Its representations, parameters, and interactive logic involve creative interpretation and should not be understood as a complete reproduction of real biological phenomena.

## Sources and Content Boundaries

When UMWELT addresses real animals, taxonomy, morphology, natural history, ecological environments, captive husbandry, captive-bred lines, trade names, or related subjects, it seeks, where possible, to rely on identifiable and verifiable sources and to distinguish documented facts from research references, working judgments, visual representations, and simulation parameters used within the project.

Sources may include, but are not limited to:

- academic papers and monographs;
- taxonomic and biodiversity databases;
- publicly available materials from museums, universities, research institutions, and professional organizations;
- natural history records and species descriptions;
- captive husbandry records and long-term observations;
- information concerning captive-bred lines, trade names, and related circulation;
- publicly accessible images, videos, and other visual references;
- other external materials with identifiable provenance.

Sources differ in reliability, purpose, and scope. The project attempts to cross-check information where reasonably possible, but the use of a source does not constitute endorsement of all content in that source, nor does it imply that the information is immune from later revision, taxonomic change, or scholarly dispute.

For taxonomic names, species identity, geographic distribution, morphological traits, and other matters that may change with new research, the most current specialist literature and authoritative databases should be treated as the primary reference.


### Factual Materials and Creative Content

The project seeks to distinguish among the following categories:

- **Documented facts**: information supported by papers, databases, formal records, or other verifiable sources;
- **Research and visual references**: materials used to understand real organisms or support visual design;
- **Trade and captive-breeding information**: captive lines, trade names, or working names used in commerce;
- **Working judgments**: provisional interpretations made for organization, production, or research when evidence is incomplete;
- **Visual parameters**: representational values used for procedural drawing, proportional consistency, or visual identification;
- **Interactive parameters**: values used for operation, pacing, and interaction logic;
- **Creative content**: elements that are explicitly fictional, designed, or artistic.

These categories should not be treated as interchangeable.

Visual parameters are not morphological measurements; interactive parameters are not results of animal-behavior experiments; captive-breeding or trade names are not automatically formal taxonomic names.


### Simulation Parameters

Temperature, humidity, movement speed, growth ratios, activity states, behavioral probabilities, environmental changes, and similar values may be simplified or adjusted for presentation, interaction pacing, or technical implementation.

Such values should not be interpreted as:

- precise biological measurements;
- experimental data;
- complete simulations of natural environments;
- deterministic descriptions of a particular species’ behavior;
- animal husbandry standards;
- professional husbandry, veterinary, or ecological advice.

Anyone carrying out actual animal husbandry, breeding, transport, research, or other practical activities should consult reliable professional sources separately and make decisions according to the relevant species, environment, and individual conditions.


### External Materials

Papers, databases, websites, images, videos, natural history materials, trade information, and other external sources cited, linked, or referenced by the project are used only for research, fact-checking, visual reference, or creative assistance.

Copyright, database rights, trademark rights, licenses, and other applicable rights in such materials remain with their respective authors, institutions, platforms, or rights holders.

Citation, linking, organization, or use as a reference does not relicense third-party material under the UMWELT project license.

Generative AI output is not treated as a scientific source and is not used as the final authority for taxonomy, natural history, biology, or other factual claims.

- original research papers;
- formal publications;

- museum or university materials;
